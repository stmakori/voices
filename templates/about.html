Our Mission: Ending GBV in Kenya

Voices Against Violence is a comprehensive online platform dedicated to combating GBV in Kenya. We provide a safe, confidential way to share reports, while educating communities and connecting survivors of all genders with essential support services.

Safe and Confidential Reporting

Our secure reporting system allows survivors and witnesses of all genders to share reports safely and anonymously, with confidentiality protected.

Comprehensive Education

We provide educational resources about GBV, prevention strategies, legal rights, and support options to empower communities.

  • 24/7 reporting available when you need it most
  • Nationwide resource finder for police posts, safe houses, and support services
  • Free educational materials and awareness campaigns
  • Connection to professional support services and counseling
Share a Report

Working Together for Change

We collaborate with government agencies, NGOs, community organizations, and support services across Kenya to provide comprehensive GBV response and prevention.
