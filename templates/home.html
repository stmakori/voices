Voices Against Violence: Ending GBV in Kenya

Gender-Based Violence (GBV) affects people of all genders in Kenya. Our platform offers a safe, confidential way to share reports while learning about prevention and support.

Everyone deserves to live free from violence. Through reporting, education, and resource access, we are building a safer Kenya for all.
