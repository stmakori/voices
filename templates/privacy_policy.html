Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your personal data and keeping your information confidential.

  1. Home
  2. Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We may collect account details, contact information, report submissions, and technical usage information needed to run and secure the platform.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide GBV reporting, support, and resource discovery services.
  • To respond to support requests and contact form submissions.
  • To improve performance, security, and user experience.
  • To meet legal, safety, and compliance requirements.

3. Confidentiality and Sensitive Reports

We treat GBV-related submissions as sensitive information. Access is limited to authorized personnel and systems needed to provide support.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data only when necessary for service operation, legal compliance, or immediate safety response.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for operational, legal, and safety purposes, then securely delete or anonymize it where applicable.

6. Security Measures

We apply technical and organizational controls to reduce unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss of personal data.

7. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to legal and safety constraints.

8. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact us via the Contact page.
